# Privacy · 隐私说明

HoverCue processes selected audio, speech recognition, translation, and optional speech playback on your Mac. It never saves audio and does not capture screen pixels. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or app network access. macOS may download Apple-managed language resources when you first prepare a language.

Text history starts off each time the app opens. To save new text, you must enable history and confirm that everyone involved knows about and agrees to the recording. Disabling history immediately stops accepting new text. Existing entries stay in the local app container until you delete them. If you export a TXT file, you choose where it goes. A synced folder may sync that file through another service.

HoverCue requests speech recognition and the system audio or microphone permission needed for your selected audio source. Microphone permission is requested only when you expressly select a microphone or ask to grant access.

## 简体中文

HoverCue 在你的 Mac 上处理所选音源、语音识别、翻译和可选的译文朗读。应用不保存音频、不捕获屏幕画面，也没有账号、分析、广告或应用联网权限。首次准备语言时，macOS 可能下载由 Apple 提供的资源。

每次打开应用时，文字历史默认关闭。只有手动启用并确认相关参与者知晓且同意后，应用才会保存新产生的文字。关闭后立即停止接收新文字，已有记录保存在应用的本地容器中，可随时删除。手动导出 TXT 时由你选择位置，存入同步文件夹可能由其他服务同步。

HoverCue 根据所选音源申请语音识别和系统音频或麦克风权限。只有你明确选择麦克风或主动申请授权后，应用才会请求麦克风权限。
